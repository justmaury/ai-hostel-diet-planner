AI Hostel Diet Planner

Building AI course project

Summary

This project uses Artificial Intelligence to help hostel students create affordable and healthy diet plans. The system recommends meals based on budget, available food, fitness goals, and nutritional needs.

Background

Many hostel students struggle to maintain a healthy diet because hostel food is often unhealthy or lacks proper nutrition. Students who go to the gym or want to stay fit face additional challenges due to low budgets and limited cooking options.

Problems this project solves:

* Poor hostel nutrition
* Difficulty maintaining fitness goals
* Limited budget for healthy food
* Lack of personalized diet guidance

My personal motivation comes from seeing students struggle with unhealthy eating habits and poor meal planning in hostels.

How is it used?

Students can enter:

* Their budget
* Fitness goal (muscle gain, fat loss, maintenance)
* Available food items
* Weight and age

The AI system will then recommend:

* Daily meal plans
* Protein intake suggestions
* Affordable food alternatives
* Simple hostel-friendly recipes

The system can be used through a mobile app or website.

Data sources and AI methods

Data Sources

* Nutrition databases
* Hostel food menus
* User fitness information
* Food price data

AI Methods

* Machine Learning
* Recommendation Systems
* Logistic Regression
* Neural Networks

Challenges

This project may face challenges such as:

* Inaccurate food data
* Different hostel food quality
* Limited internet access
* Difficulty tracking exact calorie intake

The system also cannot replace professional medical or dietary advice.

What next?

In the future, this project could:

* Support multiple languages
* Include AI image recognition for food
* Connect with fitness trackers
* Provide gym workout recommendations
* Become a complete student health assistant app
